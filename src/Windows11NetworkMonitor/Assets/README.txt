Design: rounded-square landscape gradient (#0E74E9 → #7C3AED), cyan down-arrow
and orange up-arrow (matching the graph legend) with a white pulse line.
All raster sizes were generated with Pillow (supersampled at 1024px and
LANCZOS-downscaled) from the SVG concept.
